# Changelog

## [0.2.0]

- Reminders before a meeting starts, as a banner with a Join button or a full-screen reminder with Join, Snooze and Dismiss. Off by default, with a lead time of 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes and an optional sound.
- Hide a recurring meeting from the menu bar title from its details submenu. It stays in the dropdown.
- Markers for meetings that overlap or run back-to-back, and a summary line under Today: number of meetings, busy time and when you're free.
- A Join item for each meeting when two are running at the same time, and a badge next to the menu bar icon.
- A progress ring in the menu bar icon while a meeting is in progress.
- Tomorrow's first meeting in the dropdown once today is done.
- An All calendars toggle in Settings > Calendars to turn every calendar on or off at once.

## [0.1.0]

- Next meeting and countdown in the menu bar, shown within a configurable threshold.
- Today's meetings in a native dropdown, with per-event details, copy link and open in Calendar.
- One-click join for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Right-click the status item or use a global hotkey.
- Calendar sources from macOS Calendar (EventKit), with per-calendar selection.
- Settings: countdown threshold, max title length, hide meeting title for screen sharing, launch at login, hotkey recorder.
- Automatic updates via Sparkle.
